Support

Stuck, or found a mistake in a lesson? Email us. We usually reply within a day.

How do reviews work?

Each word you learn comes back for review just before you're likely to forget it. Get it right and the next review comes later: 1 day, then 3 days, then longer each time. Get it wrong and the word comes back in about 10 minutes. The Review tab shows how many words are due today.

My daily reminder didn't show up

Open your iPhone's Settings → Notifications → Finspeak and turn on Allow Notifications. Then open Finspeak, tap Me, and pick a reminder time.

How do I start over?

Tap Me → Start over. This erases your progress on this phone and can't be undone.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Finspeak has no accounts, so your progress is saved on your phone only. If you set up your new phone from a backup of your old one, your progress usually comes with it.

Where do the examples come from?

From Akshay Ramachandran's posts, videos and calls. Each word card has a link to its source.

Is this investment advice?

No. Finspeak teaches what finance words mean. It doesn't tell you what to buy or sell.
