Say it like a pro

Stop nodding along.

Most money talk is just a few dozen words. Learn them and you can follow the meeting, read the offer letter, and ask the right question.

✕ Instead of

“They make a ton of money.”

✓ Say

“They bring in $2 billion of revenue and keep $200 million as profit.”

Why

Revenue is all the money from sales. Profit is what’s left after every cost is paid.

✕ Instead of

“The stock is cheap.”

✓ Say

“It trades at 12 times earnings.”

Why

You pay $12 for every $1 of profit the company makes in a year. This is the P/E ratio.

✕ Instead of

“My advisor takes a small cut.”

✓ Say

“I pay a 1% fee. On $200,000, that’s $2,000 a year.”

Why

A management fee is a share of all your money, every year, whether it grows or not. 1% × $200,000 = $2,000.

✕ Instead of

“Work gives me free money for retirement.”

✓ Say

“My employer matches 4% in my 401(k).”

Why

If you put 4% of your pay into your retirement account, your job adds another 4% on top.

✕ Instead of

“The company is worth $10 billion.”

✓ Say

“Its market cap is $10 billion.”

Why

Market cap is the share price times the number of shares. It’s what all the shares are worth together.
