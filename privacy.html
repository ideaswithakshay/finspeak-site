Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Finspeak teaches the words of finance in short lessons. This page covers the Finspeak app and this website.

The app

Your account

When you first open the app, it makes an account for you in the background. You don't type a name, an email or a password. The account is a random number. If you tap Sign in with Apple, that account is linked to your Apple ID so your progress can move to a new phone. We ask Apple for no name and no email.

What we store

We save a copy of your progress to your account: the words you've learned and when they're due, your level, your goals, your daily goal, your reminder time, your streak and your points. It is kept on Google Firebase. Only your account can read it.

Usage data

We count how the app is used, tied to your account number and not your name: which onboarding screens you see, which lessons you start and finish, and which answers are right or wrong. We use PostHog for this. It helps us see where lessons are too hard and where people stop. We don't sell it, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Tap Me → Delete account. That deletes your account and the progress saved to it, and wipes the progress on your phone. To erase only the progress on your phone, tap Me → Start over.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, your phone sets it up and shows it. We never see it.

Links to other sites

Some lessons link to the post or video the example came from, on Substack, YouTube or X. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

What Apple may share with us

If you chose to share data with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may send us crash reports and usage counts, like how many people opened the app. These don't include your name.

This website

What we keep

If you join the waitlist, we store your email, the level you picked, and which phone you use. We also count page visits and lesson taps, tied to a random visitor number, not your name.

What we use it for

Only to tell you about Finspeak and to learn which levels people want most. We don't sell it or share it.

Deleting it

Reply to any Finspeak email and ask, and we'll delete your details.

Children

The waitlist is not meant for children under 13.

Not advice

Finspeak teaches what finance words mean. It is not investment advice.

Questions

Email us, or see the support page.
